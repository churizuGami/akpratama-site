You've earned the promotion, the title and the trust. And you still wait for the day someone figures out you've been winging it. Every win gets explained away as luck. Every mistake gets filed permanently.

Positive thinking bounces right off a skeptical mind. So this book takes a different approach: instead of trying to feel confident, you build a case. Five minutes a day, you write down specific, dated proof of your competence, until the fraud voice runs out of arguments.

About the author

A. K. Pratama

A. K. Pratama writes practical books for high-achievers who quietly feel like frauds. Pratama means "first" in Indonesian, a reminder that every case starts with a single piece of evidence.

The books skip the pep talks and hand you a system: write down the evidence, and let the record speak.

These books are practical tools, not therapy. If self-doubt is tangled up with ongoing anxiety or depression, talking to a mental health professional is a smart move.
